Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-01-01

Summary

Fasting Lane stores your data locally on your device by default. Optional smart tools send photos or text to our servers and AI providers for on-demand processing.

Data We Store

Fasting sessions, eating window notes, optional calorie values, settings, and saved thumbnails are stored on your device.

Data We Process for Smart Tools

If you use smart features (meal photo, "If I eat this" portion coach, fridge ideas), your photo or text is sent to Fasting Lane servers and processed by OpenAI to generate results. We do not store your images on our servers.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, locations, health identifiers, or advertising IDs.

Third-Party Services

Fasting Lane uses OpenAI to process smart tool requests and USDA FoodData Central for calorie lookups. No analytics or advertising SDKs are used.
