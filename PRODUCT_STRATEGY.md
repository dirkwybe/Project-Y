# Product Strategy - Fasting Lane

## Positioning
Fasting Lane is a calm, minimal intermittent fasting companion with optional AI tools that help users stay on track without adding guilt or friction.

## Pricing Strategy
Recommended model: Free app with a paid subscription that unlocks AI features.

Why this model:
- AI calls have ongoing costs; subscription revenue aligns with usage.
- Lower install friction improves growth and word-of-mouth.
- Clear value split: core tracking is free, AI tools are premium.
- Avoids user pushback of paid app + subscription.

## Proposed Tiers
### Free
- Fasting timer + adjustments
- Eating window tracking
- Manual notes and calories
- History and insights
- Reminders (fasting, hydration)

### Pro (AI)
- Photo meal scan (food + calories)
- "If I eat this" text estimate
- Portion coach
- Fridge ideas (photo + calorie limit)
- Goal tuning recommendations

## Recommended Prices
- Monthly: $4.99 / EUR 4.99 / GBP 4.99
- Yearly: $39.99 / EUR 39.99 / GBP 39.99 (20-25% discount vs monthly)
- Trial: 7-day free trial on monthly plan only

## App Store Notes
- Use "free + subscription" in App Store Connect.
- Clearly label AI features as Pro in the UI.
- Include "Restore purchases" and "Manage subscription" links.
- List subscription terms in Settings.

## Feature Gating Rules (Recommendation)
- AI entry points show a paywall if user is not subscribed.
- Existing AI results remain visible if a subscription expires.
- Provide a single test scan or AI estimate during onboarding (optional).

## Metrics to Track
- Free to trial conversion rate
- Trial to paid conversion rate
- Monthly vs yearly mix
- AI feature usage per active subscriber
- Churn (overall + first 30 days)

## Roadmap (Enhancements)
Short term:
- Improve scan editing UX (count, name, portion correction)
- Faster AI response and better confidence messaging
- Paywall experiments (trial on/off, monthly price tests)

Mid term:
- HealthKit integration (read fasting windows, hydration, weight)
- Push notification personalization
- Multi-language support (start with Dutch + English)

Long term:
- Cross-device sync and account login
- Nutrition database integration (USDA + regional sources)
- Community challenges and streaks
- Personalized fasting goals and adaptive protocols
