Promotional text (<=170 chars)
Track fasting with calm focus, log meals, and use optional smart photo tools to estimate calories or get fridge-based meal ideas.

Description
Fasting Lane is a calm, minimalist intermittent fasting companion. Track fasts, eating windows, and mindful notes. Stay consistent with gentle reminders and weekly insights.

Features
- Start/stop fasting with one tap and adjust times anytime
- Eating window notes with optional calories and daily goal
- Smart reminders, hydration nudges, and customizable schedules
- Weekly insights and goal tuning suggestions
- Smart tools (optional, requires internet):
  - Meal photo estimates with editable items
  - "If I eat this" text estimates
  - Portion coach tips
  - Fridge photo meal ideas under a calorie limit

Privacy
All core data is stored locally on your device. Smart tools send photos or text to our servers and AI provider only when you use them.
